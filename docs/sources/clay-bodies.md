# Clay bodies, slips and standard clay data sheets: sources

Sources for `lib/catalog/bodies.ts` (`BODIES`, `BODY_CLASSES`, `SLIP_RANGES`, `BODY_CHECKS`, `MATERIAL_CLASSES`, `FIT_TESTS`), the clay-mineral additions in `lib/catalog/densities.ts` (`kyanite`, `pyrophyllite`), the six body and slip records added in `0.2.0`, and the `sheet` physical properties on standard clays in `lib/catalog/data/materials.ndjson`. All primary PDFs and supplier pages below were opened directly on 2026-11-25; figures not stated on the opened source are left out rather than interpolated.

## 1. Body classes, slip specific-gravity ranges, and check thresholds (`bodies.ts`)

- **Triaxial porcelain, stoneware, earthenware, flameware and raku body windows** (`BODY_CLASSES`, `BODY_CHECKS`):
  - W. G. Lawrence & R. R. West, _Ceramic Science for the Potter_, 2nd ed., Chilton, 1982 (quartz/cristobalite inversions, plastic vs. non-plastic packing, drying shrinkage above ~7.5% as a cracking threshold).
  - F. H. Norton, _Fine Ceramics: Technology and Applications_, McGraw-Hill, 1970 (triaxial whiteware and casting-slip clay/non-plastic ratios, 40–60% plastic clay window).
  - R. Hamer & F. Hamer, _The Potter's Dictionary of Materials and Techniques_, 6th ed., Bloomsbury, 2015 (body classes, added silica >= 20% in porcelain for glaze compression, bentonite ceiling <= 0.5% in casting slips).
  - T. Hansen, Digitalfire Reference Library: SHAB test (`https://digitalfire.com/test/shab`), boiling-water / ice-water (`BWIW`) and 300 °F ice-water crazing (`IWCT`, `https://digitalfire.com/test/iwct`), glaze-body bend test (`GBBT`), and casting-slip deflocculation (`https://digitalfire.com/article/understanding+the+deflocculation+process+in+slip+casting`).
  - ASTM C373, _Standard Test Methods for Determination of Water Absorption and Associated Properties by Vacuum Method for Pressed Ceramic Tiles and Glass Tiles and Boil Method for Extruded Ceramic Tiles and Non-tile Fired Ceramic Whiteware Products_ (apparent porosity `(W - D) / (W - S)` and bulk density `D / (W - S)`).

## 4. Standard clay `sheet` properties (`data/materials.ndjson`)

Each standard clay's `sheet` holds only the physical figures printed on the exact source cited in its `source` field:

- **Valentine Clays Ltd** (`Grolleg China Clay`, `Standard Porcelain China Clay`, `Molochite`, `Hyplas 71 Ball Clay`, `Hymod AT Ball Clay`): `<1 µm` / `<2 µm` ESD, sieve residues, dry modulus of rupture (`MN/m²`), specific gravity (`Molochite` 2.70), and fired water absorption, linear contraction and brightness at `1120 °C`, `1170 °C`, `1180 °C`, `1240 °C` and `1280 °C`.
- **Imerys / Laguna** (`EPK Kaolin`, June 2011 TDS): airfloated `+325` mesh residue `1.2%`, median particle size `1.0 µm`, dry MOR `300 psi`.
- **Old Hickory Clay Company** (`Wilco UPF Kaolin`, `OHC-Website-TDS.pdf`): `46% < 1 µm`, `59% < 2 µm`.
- **Digitalfire / Producer January 2013 sheets** (`OM-4 Ball Clay`, `Tile #6 Kaolin`, `Goldart`, `Glomax LL Calcined Kaolin`, `Albany Slip`): `OM-4` water of plasticity `32.8%`, dry shrinkage `6.2%`, `57% < 1 µm`, `68% < 2 µm`, dry MOR `610 psi (50:50 flint)`, Cone 5 & 10 total shrinkage and absorption; `Goldart` dry shrinkage `8.6%` and Cone `06, 1, 6, 8, 10` fired shrinkage and absorption; `Tile #6`, `Glomax LL` and `Albany Slip` wet sieve residues.
- **Christy Minerals** (`Hawthorn Bond Fireclay`, Jan 2013 TDS): dry shrinkage `8.5%` (Cone 4 bar), mesh grades `20–50`, Cone `08, 04, 4, 9` fired shrinkages.
- **Resco Products** (`Redart`, 07/30/2019 PDS): `200 mesh`, `6.0 psi` bond strength.
- **Plainsman Clays** (`Alberta Slip`, archived product page): `60 mesh`, `0.3–0.5%` on 100 mesh.
- **Continental European & Australian sheets**: Bodmer Ton `539` (`19% < 2 µm`); Carl Jäger `Kaolin 233` (`44% < 2 µm`, `0.02% > 63 µm`, density `2.6 g/ml`); Prodesco `Kaolinor 1C` & `Caolín Nacional Superfino` (Malvern wet granulometry); Goerg & Schneider `Ton 3616` (`6.7% < 1 µm`, `21.2% < 2 µm`, `7.0 N/mm²` dry strength, `1070/1140/1200 °C` firing shrinkage and water absorption); Sibelco `V161` (`69% < 2 µm`, `1% > 125 µm`); Imerys `New Zealand Halloysite` Premium (`3.8%` dry shrinkage, `10.9%` fired shrinkage at `1300 °C`, `2.9 MN/m²` dry MOR, SG `2.55`); Unimin Australia `Clay Ceram` (`1.0% max > 38 µm`, SG `2.67`).
